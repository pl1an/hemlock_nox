# Hemlock Nox
*Action roguelike game made for the Coco Code GameJam #1*

## History  
You play as Hemlock, an alchemist from a fallen dynasty. Hemlock, due to a series of unfortunate events, is currently fighting for his life.  
Hemlock fights using his concoctions, and as you survive you will unlock new ones and improve the ones you have.  
Be careful. You are an alchemist, not a warrior nor a mage. You are not immune to the effects of the substances you concoct. 
And even after you leave their areas of effect, most of them will have effects on you for some time.  

## Gameplay  
- You control Hemlock by using the WASD keys. You can also dash by using space.
- Move around the area, collecting diverse **poisons** on the ground.
- Use the **poisons** stored in your inventory to fight against the creatures. Throw them using left click.
- Hemlock has two core meters: **Health** and **Experience**.  
  - If the Health meter reaches zero Hemlock dies.
  - When the experience reachs a certain treashold Hemlock levels up. He can unlock new **poison** effects or strengthen the ones he has.
- Be careful. Making your **poisons** stronger also means that they are going to be more lethal to you, not only to your enemies.  
- Survive.
